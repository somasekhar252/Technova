Product Overview

Streamline Your B2B Sales Operations

BusinessFlow CRM replaces scattered spreadsheets and complex legacy tools with an intuitive drag-and-drop sales pipeline. Built on TechNova's high-speed cloud engine, it connects marketing lead capture forms directly to your sales reps with instant notifications.
