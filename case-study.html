Project Overview

Digitizing Multi-Campus Academic Operations

A consortium of 12 higher education campuses faced severe operational fragmentation due to legacy, disconnected on-premise software. Student records, fee collections, exam scheduling, and learning materials were managed in isolated databases, creating bottlenecks during registration periods.

TechNova was selected to architect and engineer SmartCampus—a centralized, multi-tenant cloud platform unifying student administration, academic delivery, and parent-student communications.

The Challenge

  • Peak Traffic Crashes: On-premise servers failed when 30,000+ students logged in simultaneously to check semester exam results.
  • Manual Fee Reconciliation: Tuition payment receipts required 3 to 5 business days of manual finance desk verification.
  • Lack of Mobile Access: Neither faculty nor students possessed a responsive mobile portal for assignments and attendance.

The TechNova Solution

Our cloud architecture team migrated the entire system to AWS utilizing Dockerized microservices behind an elastic load balancer. We built a React single-page frontend paired with a Node.js REST API layer and PostgreSQL database cluster with Redis caching for ultra-fast query responses.
