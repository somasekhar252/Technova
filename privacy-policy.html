Privacy Policy

Information regarding data collection, confidentiality, security practices, and user rights at TechNova.

Owner Confirmation & Legal Notice Required: This Privacy Policy is an unreviewed draft template provided for site structure demonstration. Because this frontend demonstration website does not currently transmit or store user data to an active backend server, the data practices described below represent template provisions. The website owner must verify actual data collection, analytics, and cookie practices with legal counsel prior to commercial deployment.

1. Information Collection Overview

TechNova respects client and visitor privacy. When you use our project cost estimator, contact forms, or job application portals, we collect information that you voluntarily provide, including your name, corporate email address, phone number, project specifications, and uploaded resumes.

2. Purpose of Processing

We process submitted information strictly for business communication, project estimation, engineering consultations, candidate recruitment, and service agreement evaluations. We do not sell or rent personal information to third-party marketing brokers.

3. Client Data Protection & NDA

Technical requirements, software architecture diagrams, proprietary source code, and business data shared during project discovery are treated under strict confidentiality standards. Standard Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) are executed prior to deep technical discovery upon client request.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

This website may use essential functional cookies and browser local storage to preserve form choices across multi-step wizard stages (such as the Project Cost Estimator) and enhance site navigation performance.

5. Data Retention & Rights

You may request information regarding your stored data, ask for updates, or request the deletion of candidate profiles and project enquiries by contacting our engineering compliance team at privacy@technova-demo.com.
